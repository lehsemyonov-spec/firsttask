/WHY RETURNME

Designed to be returned.

Every ReturnMe tag is built with one purpose: keeping our customers’ information safe & secure while helping lost items find their way back to them. Here’s why over 1.5 million customers are using ReturnMe.

Confidential

Your name, phone number, and address are never printed on your tag, helping protect you from fraud and identity theft. Instead, each tag uses a unique ID number that links to our secure database, keeping your personal information private while allowing us to help recover your lost item.

Save on costs

Losing your keys, luggage, laptop, or phone can cost hundreds or even thousands of dollars to replace. ReturnMe helps get your lost items back, saving you money, stress, and the headaches that come with starting over.

Lifetime service

Activate your tag once and it works for the entire life of the item. No subscriptions, no renewals, no expiry dates, ever.

Worldwide service

A global recovery network spans 150+ countries and 7+ languages. Lose something far from home and it's handled with the same care.

Reward incentive

ReturnMe offers a reward which motivates the finder to contact us as soon as possible, easing the return of your item.

24/7, 365 service

Finders can use our toll-free number as well as our website to report the lost item.
