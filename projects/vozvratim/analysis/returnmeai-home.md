Step 1
Initiate return

Step 2
Capture reason & photos

Selection of return reason, upload of smartphone photos of the item.

Step 3
AI evaluates the condition

Image and text analysis models evaluate the product condition and create a condition forecast including standardized classification (e.g. new, like new, B-grade).

Step 4
Item is relisted

The solution in brief
Peer-to-Peer Resale

Customer A sells returned goods directly to Customer B – without detour through retailer warehouse.

Automated Re-Listing

AI creates product description, condition label and price recommendation for immediate re-listing.

Intelligent Routing

Shipping routing optimized by distance, availability and demand – for minimal routes and fast processing.

Technology
Computer Vision – core innovation

Multimodal AI models (vision + text) evaluate product condition based on 2-5 smartphone photos. Basis: pre-trained foundation models (e.g. OpenAI Vision, Google Gemini), fine-tuning on return datasets. Output: condition class (A/B/C), defect description, price recommendation.

Re-Listing & Pricing

Automatic generation of product texts, condition labels and price suggestions based on AI assessment. Integration into existing shop systems via API (Shopify, WooCommerce, Shopware etc.).

Dynamic Routing

Algorithm selects optimal shipping route: directly Customer A → Customer B, or via local collection points. Optimized by distance, storage capacity and demand.

Infrastructure

Cloud-native architecture (AWS/GCP), scalable APIs, secure payment processing (Stripe, PayPal), integration of shipping service providers (DHL, Hermes etc.).
